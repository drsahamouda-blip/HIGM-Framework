# The Hamouda Informational Gravity Model (HIGM)

Welcome to the official open-source repository for the **Hamouda Informational Gravity Model (HIGM)**, a cosmological framework developed by Prof. Dr. Samir Ahmed Hamouda (Department of Physics, University of Benghazi).

HIGM redefines gravity as an emergent entropic phenomenon, bridging quantum information theory and large-scale cosmic physics.

## Core Framework Laws
1. **The Inverse Law of Density and Entropy:** Information Entropy ($S$) is inversely proportional to Mass Density ($\rho$).
   $$S \propto \frac{1}{\rho}$$
2. **The 9.05% Informational Boost:** Derived from the Shannon Entropy formula, this dynamic correction factor modifies general relativity equations to explain galactic rotation curves without relying on dark matter.
3. **Vacuum Informational Impedance:** Predicts a distinct **$4.91\ \mu\text{s}$** gravitational wave propagation latency, offering a clear target signature for LIGO/Virgo residual data templates.

This model actively addresses major anomalies in modern physics, including the **Hubble Tension** and the unexpected presence of massive early galaxies observed by the **James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)**.
